# Upstream attribution

The crate's existing `MIT OR Apache-2.0` license declaration is accompanied by
the full texts in `LICENSE-MIT` and `LICENSE-APACHE-2.0`.

Fixed/poly trimming, quality-window cuts and whole-read filtering use fastp
1.3.6 as their behavior source:
https://github.com/OpenGene/fastp/tree/23d6211d4f05d61f561899f1b7702435a4b5d408

The original fastp MIT terms and OpenGene copyright notice are preserved in
`LICENSES/fastp-MIT`. fastp is a compatibility oracle, not a linked runtime
dependency. Historical rsomics assets and operation-specific provenance are
recorded in `PROVENANCE.md`.

This file covers the product and upstream attribution. It is not an inventory
of licenses for all dependencies in a distributed native binary.
